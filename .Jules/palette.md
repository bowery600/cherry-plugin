## 2024-06-28 - Adding aria-pressed and role="group" to interactive filter buttons in server-rendered templates
**Learning:** Adding accessible interactive states like `aria-pressed` for filter toggle buttons directly in the `render.php` and its inline scripts is highly effective. It improves screen reader support for complex filtering UIs by using semantic `role="group"` on the containers and dynamic `aria-pressed` states on the buttons. Doing this in server-side templates instead of Gutenberg `save.js` helps avoid Block Validation errors and the need for complex block deprecation wrappers.
**Action:** When implementing filter groups or toggle buttons, always define their container as `role="group"` with a descriptive `aria-label`, and use `aria-pressed` to indicate their active state. Keep the ARIA state in sync with visual states (like `.active` classes) via JavaScript, and prioritize modifying `render.php` over `save.js` for these kinds of interactive enhancements.
